# Gates

## Screen criteria

A candidate must plausibly clear all of these to survive step 2:

- Reachable first customer without paid distribution
- No licence, certification, or regulatory approval needed
- Test can be built in under a week of agent work
- Cost to first signal under the monthly budget
- Not a business requiring physical inventory or premises
- Not dependent on a single platform's goodwill

Failing one is a kill. Do not argue candidates back in.

## GATE 1 — proceed to build

Manager submits, in under one page:

- The venture in one sentence
- Strongest evidence of demand, with source
- Strongest argument against, from the peer review
- What the test would be and what it costs
- Recommendation, with stated confidence

## GATE 2 — spend or publish

Required before any real-money or public action:

- Exact amount, to whom, and what it buys
- What signal it produces
- What result would mean kill
- Maximum downside if it goes badly

No agent may approve GATE 2. Ever.

## Human response format

Append to the item in `state/decisions.md`:

```
HUMAN: approved | rejected | more-info
NOTE: <optional>
DATE: YYYY-MM-DD
```

An item with no `HUMAN:` line is still blocked.
